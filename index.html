Creative Collaboration
& Development Flow

This approach is designed to support how we work together across teams, ensuring creative ideas are set up for success and land as strongly as possible with clients.

By bringing in the right people at key moments, we:
• Keep work closely aligned to the brief and client needs
• Create space for input where it adds value
• Ensure visibility across the team while maintaining clear creative ownership

Ultimately, this is about strengthening the work through collaboration, while keeping creative direction clear and focused.

1
Briefing & Alignment
Lead Account Manager (AM) briefs Beau and the creative team
Covers Objectives & KPIs, deliverables & formats, role of content within the wider strategy, key messaging & guardrails
Shared understanding of what success looks like
2
Ideation & Initial Brainstorm
Lead Creative team and Beau lead an initial brainstorm session
Focus Generating a range of ideas aligned to the brief. Exploring different creative routes.
Broad set of initial ideas
3
Internal Creative Alignment
Action Creative team refines brainstorm outputs and aligns on strongest concepts to take forward
Selection Based On Creative strength, brief alignment, client needs
Shortlist of prioritised concepts
4
Direction Shareback
Lead Beau shares selected directions with Account Manager and relevant team members (paid, strategy, etc.)
Purpose Provide visibility on direction and bring in any key considerations early
Account Team Provides input where relevant and confirmation to proceed
Aligned concepts to move into development
5
Concept Development
Lead Creative team develops selected concepts
Focus Bringing ideas to life with clear structure and execution. Preparing for internal review before client.
Developed concepts ready for feedback
6
Internal Review & Feedback
Process Concepts are shared with AM with agreed time for feedback ahead of client deadline
AM Reviews against brief and client needs. Consolidates feedback.
Creative Team Refines and updates concepts
Finalised concepts ready for client delivery
7
Final Delivery
Creative Creative team completes and prepares assets
AM Ensures Work meets brief and is ready for client presentation
Final assets delivered to client

Alignment & Escalation

What happens when things don't align — clear paths to resolution

[A] Alignment on Concepts

If there are differing views when concepts are shared:

  1. Bring in Account Director (Sam or Matt, and Beau if needed)
  2. Align through discussion and refinement
  3. If needed → escalate to Ant or Joe for final steer

[B] New Ideas Introduced Mid-Process

If new ideas or directions emerge after initial alignment:

! AM should be looped in before progressing

Ensures alignment with brief and prevents scope or strategy drift

[C] Feedback & Communication Protocol

How feedback is shared and where communication happens:

✓ All feedback on work should be:

  • Shared directly with the relevant person
  • Via Slack DM or a quick call, not public channels

■ Slack channels are for:

  • Quick questions
  • Status updates
  • Progress check-ins
  • Information where wider visibility is needed

☎ For feedback:

  • Keep it direct, clear and constructive
  • Use calls or DMs for discussion, especially where nuance is needed

■ Principle:

  • Direct conversations drive better work
  • Channels are for visibility, not critique
