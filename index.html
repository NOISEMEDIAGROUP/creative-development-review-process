Creative Development
& Review Process

How creative and account teams work together — from brief to delivery, with clear checkpoints and escalation paths.

1
Briefing & Alignment
Lead Account Manager leads a clear creative brief session with the creative team
Covers Objectives & KPIs, deliverables & formats, role of content within the wider strategy, key messaging & guardrails
Shared understanding of what success looks like
2
Ideation & Direction Setting
Lead Creative team runs a brainstorm session
Attendees AM and/or relevant strategist, paid media lead, or account team member where it makes sense
Process Ideas discussed, refined and challenged in the room. Narrowed down to a clear set of routes to progress.
Agreed creative directions to take forward
If no cross-team members were present at ideation:
3
Cross-Team Validation (If Needed)
When If no team members outside creative (account, paid, strategy) were present at ideation
Action A short follow-up session is held to review and align on directions
Ensures Ideas match the brief, channel considerations are accounted for, no misalignment later
Final confirmed routes for development
4
Midway Checkpoint Setup
Lead AM defines a midway review point — typically ~50% of time to final delivery
Purpose Sense-check one developed direction. Avoid late-stage rework.
Checkpoint scheduled at the halfway mark
5
Creative Development (WIP Review)
Stage Creative team develops work to a clear directional stage — not fully polished, but strong enough to show concept, structure and execution
Review Creative lead (e.g. Beau) alignment, then quick review with AM / strategy / paid
Outcomes
✓ Proceed ■ Minor tweaks ■ Flag gaps early
Confidence in direction before final production
6
Final Development & Delivery
Creative Creative team completes assets following the aligned direction
AM Checks Output meets brief and is ready for client delivery
Final assets delivered to client

Discrepancy & Escalation

What happens when things don't align — clear paths to resolution

[A] Post-Brainstorm Misalignment

If AM and creative team disagree on direction:

  1. Escalate to Account Director (Sam or Matt)
  2. Attempt to resolve through discussion and refinement
  3. If still unresolved → escalate to Executive Leadership (Ant or Joe) for final decision

[B] Misalignment During Development

If AM is not aligned on WIP creative:

  1. Escalate to Account Director for review
  2. Align on changes or direction
  3. If still unresolved → escalate to Ant or Joe

[C] New Ideas Introduced Mid-Process

If new creative routes or ideas emerge during development:

! AM must be consulted before progressing

Ensures alignment with brief and prevents scope or strategy drift

[D] Feedback & Communication Protocol

How feedback is shared and where communication happens:

✓ All feedback on work should be:

  • Shared directly with the relevant person via Slack DM or a quick call
  • Discussed 1:1 or in a small, relevant group where needed

✗ Avoid sharing feedback in public Slack channels

  • Channels are not for critique, opinions or directional feedback
  • This prevents confusion, noise and undermining of ownership

■ Slack channels should be used for:

  • Quick questions
  • Status updates
  • Progress check-ins
  • Information where wider visibility is needed

☎ If feedback is nuanced or requires discussion:

  • Jump on a quick call rather than long written threads
  • Align clearly before wider sharing
